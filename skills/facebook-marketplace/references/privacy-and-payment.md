# Privacy and payment

## Never in buyer-facing text, media, or shared reports

Review entered text, selected media, visible browser state, screenshots, and
saved notes before sharing or publishing. Exclude or redact:

- Receipts, order numbers, transaction references, warranty claim IDs.
- Serial numbers, unless disclosure is necessary and the seller explicitly
  approves; prefer safe model evidence.
- Street addresses, precise pickup location, personal phone numbers and email
  addresses, account names, payment details, private contacts.
- Image metadata that reveals location, device, or other personal details.
- Reflections, backgrounds, labels, screens, and documents that show a person,
  home address, workplace, contact information, or account data.
- Private pricing floors and negotiation notes.

Private documents may be read for facts, but never copied into buyer-facing
text or committed without a privacy check. Keep location and contact disclosure
to the minimum the form requires.

## Payment and handoff

Read the payment policy from project configuration or `seller.payment`; never
invent one. Use it when a buyer asks about payment or when arranging handoff,
never in listing copy. Whatever the policy says, these are fixed:

- A payment counts only when the seller sees it received in their own app, or
  in cash at handoff. Screenshots, emails, "pending" notices, and account
  upgrade requests are unverified.
- Overpay-and-refund requests, checks of any kind, money orders, gift cards,
  and shipping or payment arranged outside the platform's own checkout are
  declined; flag them to the seller as likely fraud.
- Never enter, relay, or store the seller's or a buyer's account identifiers,
  handles, or payment links in the record or the copy.
